# UI Walkthrough & Screen Specification Guide — MyHealth AI

> **Senior Project Design & Interface Specification:** University of Bahrain — College of Information Technology  
> **Target Framework:** Flutter 3.44+ / Material 3 Design System / Custom Clinical Tokens

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Visual Language

**MyHealth AI** employs a high-density, professional clinical interface designed for high-stress medical environments and clear patient communication.

### Core Visual Principles:
1. **Double-Bezel Card Elevation (`DoubleBezelCard`):** Uses an outer subtle border paired with an inner elevated surface to create physical depth without heavy, distracting drop shadows.
2. **Standardized Clinical Badge System (`ClinicalBadge`):** Color-coded status and risk chips paired with semantic icons (e.g. Critical Red, Warning Amber, Success Green, Info Blue, AI Violet).
3. **Safety Banners (`SafetyBanner`):** Mandatory clinical AI disclaimers displayed on all generative AI surfaces to maintain medical safety compliance.
4. **Adaptive Navigation:** Responsive Navigation Rail for desktop/tablet ($\ge 768\text{ dp}$) and bottom navigation bar for mobile.

## 2. Patient Experience Screens

### 2.1 Patient Home & AI Synthesis (`PatientHomeScreen`)
- **Header:** Personalized patient greeting, age, blood type, and active chronic conditions.
- **`AiSummaryCard`:** Longitudinal health synthesis generated by Claude / Mock AI, highlighting:
  - *Key Medical Events:* Significant milestones extracted across past years.
  - *Identified Trends:* Trajectory tags for Blood Pressure, Blood Glucose, and Weight.
  - *Clinical Red Flags:* Urgent observations requiring provider attention.
  - *Safety Banner:* AI disclaimer with model ID and prompt version metadata.
- **Quick Actions:** Instant shortcuts for *Book Appointment*, *Import Medical PDF*, *Log Vitals*, and *View Timeline*.

### 2.2 Unified Health Timeline (`TimelineScreen`)
- Chronological timeline combining doctor encounter notes, laboratory diagnostic reports, imaging findings, and vitals milestones.
- Filter chips: `All`, `Consultations`, `Lab Reports`, `Discharges`, `Imaging`.
- Deep linking: Tapping a record opens full clinical documentation and analyte tables.

### 2.3 Interactive Vitals & Trends (`VitalsScreen`)
- Real-time `fl_chart` time-series graphs for Systolic & Diastolic Blood Pressure, Blood Glucose, SpO2, and Heart Rate.
- "Log Vitals" floating button triggering `LogVitalsModal` with immediate clinical rule screening.

### 2.4 AI Smart Booking Wizard (`BookingWizardScreen`)
- **Step 1 — Department Selection:** Cards for Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Endocrinology, Pulmonology, Nephrology.
- **Step 2 — Doctor Selection:** Clinician profiles with room numbers and specialty.
- **Step 3 — AI Optimal Slot Ranking:** Candidate 30-minute slots ranked by lowest ML predicted no-show risk ($P \in [0, 1]$), displaying `Low Risk`, `Medium Risk`, or `High Risk` badges and `★ Top` recommendations.
- **Step 4 — Confirmation & Feature Attribution:** Displays explainable AI chips showing the top contributing factors (e.g. *"Lead time (+14%)"*, *"Morning slot (-8%)"*) and the automated reminder schedule.

### 2.5 My Appointments (`PatientAppointmentsScreen`)
- Tabbed/separated view of Upcoming and Past appointments.
- Status badges: `Booked`, `Checked In`, `Completed`, `Cancelled`, `No-Show`.
- One-click **Reschedule** (re-opens Booking Wizard) and **Cancel** actions.

## 3. Clinician & Staff Experience Screens

### 3.1 Staff Daily Dashboard (`StaffDashboardScreen`)
- **Active Risk Flags Ticker:** High-visibility banner highlighting unacknowledged critical emergencies (e.g. *"2 Critical Clinical Risk Flags requiring immediate review"*).
- **Daily Appointment List:** Time-ordered patient queue with ML no-show risk badges and quick status buttons: `Check In`, `No-Show`, `Complete`.
- Tapping a patient navigates directly into their **Clinical Patient Chart**.

### 3.2 Clinical Task Board (`TaskBoardScreen`)
- Filterable task board (`Pending`, `In Progress`, `Completed`, `All`).
- Tasks sorted by blended score ($40\%$ Rule Urgency $+ 60\%$ AI Model Priority).
- **AI Explainability Rationale Box:** Displays clear medical justification for priority ranking.
- Quick action buttons to start and complete tasks.

### 3.3 Patient Search Directory (`PatientSearchScreen`)
- Real-time instant search by Patient Name, CPR / National ID, or Email.
- Chronic disease filter chips (`Type 2 Diabetes`, `Hypertension`, `Asthma`, `Dyslipidemia`).

### 3.4 Clinical Patient Chart (`PatientChartScreen`)
- Comprehensive 5-tab provider view:
  1. **Overview:** Demographics, allergies, emergency contacts, active risk flags with inline acknowledge button.
  2. **AI Summary:** Full longitudinal AI synthesis and trend trajectories.
  3. **Timeline:** Filterable encounter history with embedded lab values.
  4. **Vitals & Labs:** Interactive `fl_chart` time-series trajectory and recent readings table.
  5. **Medications:** Active prescriptions, start/end dates, and prescriber names.
- Quick action header buttons: *Add Encounter Note*, *Prescribe Medication*, *Log Vitals*.

### 3.5 Clinic Analytics & Insights (`ClinicAnalyticsScreen`)
- Top KPI summary cards (Attendance Rate $78.4\%$, ML ROC-AUC $0.785$).
- Attendance distribution pie chart (Attended vs No-Show).
- Department no-show rate bar chart comparing internal medicine, cardiology, and endocrinology.

## 4. Administration & AI Governance Screens

### 4.1 Staff User Management (`UserManagementScreen`)
- Roster of all doctors, nurses, and administrative users.
- Modal to create new staff accounts with department assignment and license numbers.
- Instant switch to toggle account active/inactive status.

### 4.2 Department & Schedule Templates (`DepartmentScheduleScreen`)
- Department overview and default clinic operating hours (Sunday–Thursday, 08:00–16:00).

### 4.3 AI Configuration & Seeder (`AiSettingsScreen`)
- **Dual AI Mode Switch:** Toggle Mock AI (offline defense mode) vs Anthropic Claude API.
- **Model Selector:** Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3.5 Haiku, Claude 3 Opus.
- **API Key Entry:** Securely persisted Anthropic API key with visibility toggle.
- **Database Re-Seeder Button:** One-click reset generating 60 synthetic patient personas and 2 years of history.

### 4.4 System Audit Trail (`AuditLogScreen`)
- Searchable and filterable log of system actions (`LOGIN`, `CREATE_NOTE`, `PRESCRIBE`, `BOOK_APPT`, `ACKNOWLEDGE_FLAG`) with actor names and timestamps.
